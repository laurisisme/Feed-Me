# 🍜 FEEDME

### *You don't know what to eat. FEEDME does.*

**FEEDME** is a tiny interactive game that helps you decide what to eat today — except instead of simply asking you what you want, it tries to **figure you out**.

Answer 3 random questions, survive a few ridiculous mini-games, and let FEEDME analyze the evidence.

At the end, you'll get **one food. No rerolls. No overthinking. Just eat.**

---

## 🎮 How It Works

**1. Answer 3 questions**

FEEDME randomly picks three questions about your mood, hunger, budget, energy, food cravings, and willingness to leave the house.

> “How hungry are you?”

> “How are we doing?”

> “How much effort are we putting into this meal?”

---

**2. Play tiny mini-games**

Instead of making you answer endless questions, FEEDME makes you *do* things.

Depending on the playthrough, you might have to:

* 🍴 Mash the keyboard because you're hungry
* 🍜 Catch falling food
* 🌶️ Test your spice tolerance
* ⌨️ Make rapid-fire food decisions
* 👨‍🍳 Time your cooking
* 🧠 Trust your first instinct

Your choices and performance quietly contribute to your food profile.

---

**3. Let FEEDME judge you**

After collecting enough “evidence,” FEEDME analyzes your answers and gameplay.

> Hunger level: ✓ Concerning
> Financial situation: ✓ Complicated
> Willingness to leave the house: ✓ Absolutely not
> Life choices: ✓ Interesting

Then comes...

# THE VERDICT

One food is selected from a pool of **20 possible results**.

No multiple choices.

No reroll.

No “maybe try something else.”

**FEEDME has spoken.**


---

## 📢 And Then It Gets Worse

After revealing your food, FEEDME gives you a completely fictional **personalized restaurant advertisement**.

Because apparently deciding what to eat wasn't enough.

For example:

> ### 🍜 RAMEN HOUSE™
>
> *“For when your problems require noodles.”*
>
> ★★★★★ 4.9
> 18–27 min
>
> **ORDER MY DESTINY →**

Clicking the button may result in...

> **ORDER CONFIRMED.**
>
> *Just kidding. 😭*

The restaurants, ratings, delivery times, and advertisements are intentionally fictional.

## ✨ Design Philosophy

FEEDME started from a simple question:

> **Can deciding what to eat feel more like a game than a decision?**

Rather than building another recommendation interface with a long list of options, FEEDME turns a mundane everyday decision into a small interactive experience.

The goal is not to find the objectively “correct” food.

It's to make the player think:

> **“Okay... that's actually what I want.”**

## 🧠 What I Experimented With

This project was also an experiment in **vibe coding** — using AI to rapidly turn a playful interaction concept into a working browser experience.

Instead of starting from the question:

> “What can I technically build?”

I started from:

> **“What would make this tiny interaction actually fun?”**

The development process focused on experimenting with:

* lightweight game mechanics
* keyboard-based interactions
* randomized gameplay
* hidden scoring systems
* micro-interactions and feedback
* comedic UI writing
* AI-assisted prototyping
